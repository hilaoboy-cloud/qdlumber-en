Engineered Modular Crates

Knockdown Crate Kits

Engineered OEM flat-pack packaging systems combining precision wood panels and structural metal components for efficient on-site assembly at destination.

  • Each kit includes pre-cut panels, reinforced frames, metal fittings, braces, and assembly hardware, ensuring fast installation and consistent structural performance across deployment.
  • Optimized for container utilization — these systems reduce shipping volume by up to 60% while maintaining full structural strength and protection performance after assembly.
  • Standardized numbering and interlocking hardware allow rapid assembly by overseas operators with standard hand tools.
