Flat-Pack Architecture

Knock-Down (KD) Crate Kits

Flat-pack wooden crate systems engineered for space efficiency and fast on-site assembly.

  • Designed to reduce shipping and storage volume where suitable, lowering ocean freight and domestic transport costs.
  • Pre-drilled and pre-fitted panels designed for accurate fit and secure assembly at destination.
  • Suitable for recurring equipment programs, returnable loops, or projects requiring streamlined field crating.

Structural Reinforcement

Reinforced Framing & Hardware Integration

Heavy-duty timber frames integrated with structural steel brackets and corner hardware for high-load applications.

  • Designed for accurate fit and secure assembly, reinforcing key stress points during lifting and multi-modal transport.
  • Configured with heavy-duty skids, lifting provisions, and forklift entry pockets for safe handling.
  • Surface-treated hardware options including galvanizing or zinc plating available based on project needs.
Hardware Integration: High-Strength Corner Brackets & Lifting Hardware
Export Compliance

Export Packaging Preparation & ISPM 15 Support

Solid wood heat treatment and documentation to support international trade requirements.

  • Applicable solid wood packaging materials undergo certified heat treatment meeting ISPM 15 standards.
  • Official IPPC stamps applied clearly on exterior crate surfaces for customs verification.
  • Support applicable export documentation and destination-country phytosanitary requirements.
Export Readiness: ISPM 15 Compliant Solid Wood & IPPC Marking
