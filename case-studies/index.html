Packaging Applications

Examples of wood packaging applications across industrial and technology sectors. Each configuration is adapted to the product, handling method and shipping requirements.

Application Examples Across Sectors

A representative overview of wood packaging configurations developed to customer specifications.

Application Example

Server & Data Center Equipment

Context: Export and domestic shipping of rack servers, computing clusters and high-value electronics.

Packaging Requirements: Vibration cushioning, internal stability during multi-modal transit, and ramp access for safe uncrating.

Configuration: Custom timber frame with shock-mitigating foam inserts and integrated entry ramp.

Delivered Scope: Tailored wooden crates supplied per project batch schedules.

Application Example

High-Voltage Insulators

Context: Bulk international shipment of fragile glass, ceramic and porcelain insulator discs.

Packaging Requirements: Separation between components, impact mitigation, and heavy stacking strength during sea freight.

Configuration: Compartmentalized crate structure with protective dividers preventing component-to-component contact.

Delivered Scope: Reinforced export crates configured for containerized ocean transit.

Application Example

Industrial Machinery

Context: Capital equipment transport including machine tools, industrial printers, pumps and compressors.

Packaging Requirements: High load-bearing capacity, forklift access, and structural rigidity for irregular centers of gravity.

Configuration: Heavy-duty hardwood skids, reinforced corner framing, and customized internal blocking.

Delivered Scope: Custom-engineered machinery crates manufactured to equipment weight and dimensions.

Application Example

Energy Equipment

Context: Battery enclosures, EV charging stations, transformers and electrical distribution units.

Packaging Requirements: Secure base anchoring, moisture barrier protection, and compliance with destination transport requirements.

Configuration: Engineered base framing with weather barrier wrap and bolted internal tie-down brackets.

Delivered Scope: Project-specific crates configured according to product dimensions and transport mode.

Application Example

Knock-Down Packaging Kits

Context: Recurring OEM equipment manufacturing requiring space-efficient component supply.

Packaging Requirements: Compact shipping volume before assembly and quick, standardized assembly at destination facility.

Configuration: Pre-drilled plywood and timber panels with metal corner brackets for flat-pack transport.

Delivered Scope: Flat-pack KD kits delivered in bulk, designed to reduce shipping volume where suitable.

Application Example

Custom Export Crating

Context: International break-bulk and containerized cargo requiring certified wood packaging.

Packaging Requirements: Full ISPM 15 compliance, IPPC stamps, durable transit protection under rough sea conditions.

Configuration: Heat-treated solid lumber and plywood construction with weather-resistant internal liners.

Delivered Scope: Export-ready wooden crates delivered with required phytosanitary treatment marks.
