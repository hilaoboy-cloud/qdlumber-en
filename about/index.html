About Qingdao Lumber & Packaging

In today's global supply chains, packaging is no longer just protection — it is a critical part of operational stability, cost control, and delivery efficiency.

Qingdao Lumber & Packaging was established to support this demand with reliable OEM manufacturing, precise engineering execution, and scalable production capability.

We ensure that every component, system, and shipment is delivered with consistency and full alignment to customer requirements.

Qingdao Lumber & Packaging Co., Ltd. is a U.S.-owned OEM packaging manufacturer with full-scale production facilities in Qingdao, China.

For 20+ years, we have combined international quality expectations with efficient manufacturing capabilities to serve industrial clients across North America, Europe, and global markets.

We specialize in custom knockdown crate systems, export packaging crates, and industrial protective packaging solutions.

All products are ISPM-15 compliant and fully prepared for international shipment.

To date, we have delivered over 5 million crate sets, supporting global industrial and technology supply chains with stable and scalable OEM production.

Our customers face increasing cost pressure, supply chain complexity, and the need for consistent quality across global operations.

We design OEM packaging systems that address these challenges through standardized engineering, scalable production, and optimized logistics performance.

We manufacture precision-cut wood components and fully integrated knockdown crate systems based on customer drawings and technical requirements.

Each OEM kit includes structural elements, metal fittings, fasteners, and protective materials, all matched and assembled into complete transport-ready systems for efficient deployment.

We operate as an integrated extension of our customers' supply chain, supporting OEM programs with structured processes, repeatable quality, and reliable global delivery.

From engineering review to final shipment, every stage is managed with precision and consistency.

From Drawing to Delivery

Seven controlled stages take every OEM packaging program from engineering review to final shipment.

Step 01

Engineering Design

Every project starts with engineering — our team designs crate systems in 3D CAD from your drawings and specifications.

Step 02

Material Selection

Certified timber and ISPM-15 compliant materials, selected and inspected for strength and export readiness.

Step 03

Precision Processing

CNC machining, precision cutting and drilling — every component produced to exact tolerances.

Step 04

Kit Assembly

Panels, frames, metal fittings and fasteners are matched and pre-assembled into complete knockdown kits.

Step 05

Quality Inspection

Strict quality inspection at every stage — dimensional checks, structural verification and export compliance.

Step 06

Packaging & Logistics

Export-ready packing with protective materials, moisture barriers and complete shipping documentation.

Step 07

Delivery & Support

Container loading and global delivery, with responsive support from order to arrival.
