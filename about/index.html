About Qingdao Lumber & Packaging

Custom wood packaging manufacturing and repeat OEM program support in Qingdao, China.

Qingdao Lumber & Packaging supports industrial customers with custom wood packaging and OEM manufacturing in China.

We work with customer drawings, product specifications and shipping requirements to produce packaging for industrial equipment and export shipments. Our focus is on practical manufacturing, consistent production and responsive project coordination.

As part of a broader business network, we work to support customer requirements through clear communication and long-term supply relationships.

PRODUCTION FACILITY

Jimo Production Base · Qingdao

Dedicated wood packaging manufacturing facility in Jimo, Qingdao, equipped with automated cutting lines, CNC routing equipment, component assembly bays, and certified ISPM 15 heat treatment kilns.
